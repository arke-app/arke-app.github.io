Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · applies to Arke for iPhone and Android

Arke is made by Miguel Gonçalves. Arke has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and the trafficking of children. Using Arke for any of these is forbidden by our Terms of Use.

How Arke works

Arke is a private group chat for people who already know each other: there are no public profiles, no search for other users, no public feeds and no way to message strangers. A person can only join a chat with its QR code or invite link, given by someone in it. Arke is meant for people aged 13 and over.

Messages are end-to-end encrypted on the phone: the developer can't read them, and they pass through the relay server sealed. This protects everyone's privacy, and it means reports from users are the way abuse comes to our attention.

How to report

  • In the app: ⚙︎ Settings → About Arke → Report a Problem opens an email to the developer. Include what happened and, if you can, screenshots. You can leave any chat at once (chat → ⓘ → Delete Chat), and delete any message from your phone.
  • By email: miguelvirt@gmail.com (subject “Arke child safety report”). Reports are read and answered as a priority.
  • To the authorities: if a child is in immediate danger, call 112 (or your local emergency number). You can also report CSAM to NCMEC CyberTipline or to the INHOPE hotline in your country.

What we do

  • Every report is reviewed. Where we can identify relay traffic tied to a report (for example a chat's topic on our relay server), we stop it from being relayed and remove what the server still holds.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant authorities, as the law requires, and cooperate with law enforcement requests that follow due legal process.
  • We comply with the child safety laws that apply to Arke, including those of Portugal and the European Union.
