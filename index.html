More ways to keep talking.

After a storm, an earthquake or a long blackout, calls and data often stop working. Arke sends every message every way it can at the same time — text message, internet, phones nearby and Meshtastic® radio — so your group has more chances to keep talking. Every message is end-to-end encrypted on your phone first.

Four ways through

Text message, internet, phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi with no network at all, and Meshtastic LoRa radio for kilometres. Each needs something to work — a signal, a phone in range, a radio within reach — so none is a promise; together they give you more chances.

Join with a QR code

No account and no phone number needed. Show the chat's QR code or send its link; iPhone and Android in the same chat.

Like the chats you know

Photos, files, voice messages, your location, reactions, replies, edits — and deleting a message for everyone.

A map for the group

Where everyone is, how they are (OK, Danger, Help), and pins for shelter, water, first aid and meeting points. Save map areas so the streets show without internet.

Private by design

Each chat has its own key, made on the phone, and can use its own relay server: NeedToApp's, ntfy.sh or yours. The relay, the radios and the phones that carry messages can't read them.

Free, then one payment

Free, with everything, for one encrypted chat — no limit on people or messages. The Full Version, a single payment of €4.99, unlocks as many chats as you like.

Not an emergency service

Arke helps your group stay in touch, but it doesn't replace the emergency services. If you or someone else is in danger, call 112 (or your local emergency number) whenever you can.
