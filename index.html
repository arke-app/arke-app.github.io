More ways to keep talking.

After a storm, an earthquake or a long blackout, calls and data often stop working. Arke gives your group more ways to keep talking — text message, the internet and phones nearby, and Meshtastic® radio — each in its own tab in every chat, so you choose how each message goes. Every message is end-to-end encrypted on your phone first.

Three tabs, more ways through

SMS needs only a mobile signal. Data goes over the internet, or phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi with no network at all. Mesh goes kilometres on a Meshtastic LoRa radio. Each needs something to work — a signal, a phone in range, a radio within reach — so none is a promise; together they give you more chances.

Encrypted SMS, no internet needed

To text just one person, your phone’s own messages app is the simplest way. Arke’s SMS is for the group: messages sealed so only the chat can read them, the whole chat keeps talking after the internet is gone, and your location, how you are and map pins go too. Each SMS carries your last 3, so tapping the latest catches up.

Your privacy

  • Encrypted on your phone. Every message, photo, location and pin is encrypted before it leaves, with a key only the people in the chat have.
  • No account. No email, no sign-up. Your phone number is optional, only for SMS.
  • On your phone only. Your chats, keys and messages live on your phone. Delete the app and they're gone.
  • Sealed on the way. The relay, the radios and the phones that pass messages on carry them sealed: they can't open them, and neither can we. Each chat can use NeedToApp's relay, ntfy.sh or your own.
  • Nothing watching. No ads, no analytics, no trackers, in the app or on this site.
